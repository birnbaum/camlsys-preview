Projects

A selection of the systems, methods and collaborations we are working on.

SOFAIR

We are developing systems and algorithms to train frontier AI models across wide-area networks of heterogeneous compute. Our contribution to SOFAIR explores how shared GPU infrastructure can pool resources across institutions and better manage demand on the power grid.

  • Explore SOFAIR

The Red Queen Gödel Machine

Can AI systems keep improving when the tests used to evaluate them become a bottleneck? We develop agents whose evaluators evolve alongside them, allowing both to improve beyond the limits of a fixed test.

  • Read the blog article
  • Paper

SPRIND

Through CambridgeFlower, we are extending Flower to train AI models across decentralised, heterogeneous hardware. In SPRIND’s Composite Learning Challenge, we develop scalable training systems that let organisations combine their compute while keeping data local.

  • Composite Learning Challenge

Wide-area distributed training

We develop systems and optimisation methods for training large models across geographically distributed GPUs. Our work reduces communication and memory requirements and enables collaborative training across diverse hardware and data sources.
