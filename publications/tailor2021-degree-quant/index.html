Degree-Quant: Quantization-Aware Training for Graph Neural Networks

  • Shyam A. TailorUniversity of Cambridge
  • Javier Fernandez-MarquesUniversity of Oxford
  • Nicholas D. LaneUniversity of CambridgeSamsung AI Center, Cambridge

International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR), 2021

Degree-Quant adapts quantization-aware training to graph neural networks, enabling lower-precision inference. The recording below presents an earlier version at the ICML 2020 Graph Representation Learning and Beyond Workshop.
