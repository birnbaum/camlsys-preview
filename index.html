Cambridge Machine Learning Systems Group

We develop algorithms and systems for scalable, reliable and sustainable machine learning. Our research spans training across distributed infrastructure, evaluating and improving AI behaviour, and reducing the computational and environmental costs of AI.

Our group is based in the Department of Computer Science and Technology at the University of Cambridge.

Research

Wide-Area Distributed Training

We develop algorithms and systems for training large-scale models across geographically distributed, heterogeneous infrastructure. We investigate how to overcome limits in communication, coordination and resource availability.

Agentic AI and Self-Improvement

We develop AI systems that plan, act and learn through interaction with their environment. We investigate how agents can use tools, collaborate and improve their capabilities through experience, feedback and autonomous experimentation.

AI Safety

We study how to make AI systems reliable, controllable and accountable. Our research addresses how to understand and evaluate their behaviour, manage risks, and maintain effective oversight as systems become more capable and autonomous.

Efficiency and Sustainability

We investigate how computing systems can use resources more efficiently and reduce their environmental impact. Our work spans hardware-level efficiency, energy-aware system design, and facility-wide carbon attribution schemes that incentivise grid flexibility.
