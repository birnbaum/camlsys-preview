Research

Wide-Area Distributed Training

We develop algorithms and systems to train large-scale models across geographically distributed, heterogeneous infrastructure.

Agentic AI and Self-Improvement

We build AI agents that solve complex tasks, learn from experience and improve their through experimentation and feedback.

AI Safety

As systems become more capable and autonomous, we study how to make AI reliable, controllable and accountable.

Efficiency and Sustainability

We improve computing efficiency at the hardware level and explore how datacenters can reduce their environmental impact.
